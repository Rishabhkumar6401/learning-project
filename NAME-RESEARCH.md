# Name & domain research — "chaupar" (7 Oct 2026)

Sources: Ubersuggest (keyword overview, suggestions, SERP), mcp360 (keyword-research, Google Trends, WHOIS), RDAP, npm/GitHub.

## What people mean when they search "chaupar"
Mostly NOT the game. Google Trends related topics for India (12 months): Badi Chaupar (Jaipur metro station/market) 100, Jaipur 64, Chaupar the game 54, Choti Chaupar 23, rapid transit / subway station / distance. "badi chaupar" alone has ~49,500 global searches/month.

Takeaway: "chaupar" is fine as a BRAND (short, Indian, memorable, free on npm and as a GitHub user), but it brings no relevant search traffic. Page titles/README should target "play Ludo online with friends, no download, open source".

## Existing uses of the name
- Apps: "Chaupar" (iOS, id1166009315) and "Chaupar: Pachisi Classic Game" (Android, com.ratan.chaupar). Generic traditional-game name; low conflict risk for an open-source repo, but not unique.
- GitHub: 12 repos with "chaupar" in the name; user/org `chaupar` is free. npm `chaupar` is free.

Taken: chaupar.com (registered May 2024, Spaceship/Porkbun NS, privacy — looks parked), chaupar.in (registered 1 Oct 2026, GoDaddy, registrant in Rajasthan), chausar.com, chausar.in, chaupad.com, pachisi.com, pachisi.in, pachisi.dev.
Available (checked 7 Oct 2026): chaupar.dev, chaupar.app, chaupar.games, chaupar.io, chaupar.gg, chaupar.net, chaupar.org, chaupar.online, chaupar.xyz, chaupar.fun, chaupar.live, chaupar.club, chaupar.site, playchaupar.com, chaupargame.com, chausar.dev. (chaupar.co returned unknown.)

Decision: no domain needed until Phase 6 (week 12); `chaupar.pages.dev` + Cloudflare Tunnel are ₹0. If buying later, prefer chaupar.dev or chaupar.games (both HTTPS-only TLDs, fine behind Cloudflare). Avoid .xyz/.online/.site (cheap first year, bad reputation, expensive renewals).
